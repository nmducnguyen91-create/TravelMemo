# 01 — Product blueprint
TravelMemo: My Atlas. My Passport. My Life. Personal/family archive for decades, not a social or booking platform.
Four pillars: Atlas (world/country/region/place), Passport (collectible stamps), Journeys (chapters and timelines), Memories (photos and journals). Lifetime Archive supports portability. Home surfaces recent journeys and coverage; Library contains memories, statistics and settings.
MVP: offline atlas, World/Vietnam passports, manual and foreground GPS check-in, retrospective journeys, selected photo import, companions, statistics, backup/restore. Later: reconstruction, GPS routes, family profiles, creative posters/books, annual wrapped, 3D globe, optional AI and sync. No always-on tracking by default.
Success: record a past or current journey, confirm a visit, see atlas and stamp update, attach memory, restore everything on another installation.
